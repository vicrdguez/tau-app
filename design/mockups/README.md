# Mockups

Renders of the HTML prototype built from `../spec.md` and `../fixture.json`, at 1512 × 982 in Ember dark unless the name says otherwise. They are a model of the layout, not the rune client: **the text wins over any image.** `../evidence.md` says what was checked on them.

**Status:** the calm pass after the calm challenge, rendered by `../../tools/proto/` from the states in `../../tools/proto/states.md`. Every check family passed on all 49 renders; the 800 px state is a sheet of three frames.
